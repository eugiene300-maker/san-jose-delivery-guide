# Shopping with a Friend or Caretaker: Etiquette and Consent

*Bringing support to the counter helps—if roles, privacy, and consent stay clear for everyone in the vestibule.*

Adults shop cannabis with friends for courage, translation, ride shares, or disability support. Caretakers may accompany medical patients. Etiquette keeps those visits respectful inside ID-gated rooms that already feel intense for first-timers.

Consent leads. Ask before sharing someone else’s tolerance story with staff. Ask before posting a friend on social media outside a dispensary. Ask before buying an infused edible “surprise” for a roommate. Cannabis is personal; peer pressure does not become cute at the register.

Role clarity helps security and staff. Who is purchasing? Whose ID will be checked? If only one adult is buying, the friend should not handle product that is not theirs in ways that confuse possession. California adult-use rules center age 21-plus identity verification; caretakers should follow store policy and state medical rules when applicable rather than inventing workarounds.

Inside the shop, avoid narrating your friend’s entire mental-health history to a budtender. Speak in shopping goals: lower dose, less smell, easier packaging. Let the purchaser lead unless they ask you to translate or advocate. Do not shame staff—or your friend—for saying no to a high-milligram suggestion.

After checkout, consent continues. Do not dose a friend covertly. Do not dump smoke on a caretaker who asked for odor-free formats. If you are the designated driver, stay sober. If you are the caretaker, know where products will be stored away from children and pets at home.

San Jose’s scarce shops mean waits happen. Bring water, patience, and a plan for who pays tax totals. Support is a gift when it preserves dignity. It becomes a problem when it steals privacy. In San Jose’s limited storefront market, preparation beats impulse: verify the shop on SJPD’s registered cannabis businesses list, bring ID, read the package yourself after any recommendation, and keep consumption on private property with permission. Adult-use sales require customers 21 and older. Tax totals at checkout still apply. None of those retail realities replace clinician advice when health questions are personal.
