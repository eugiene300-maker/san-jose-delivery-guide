# Wedding and Party Favors: Legal Limits and Host Liability Basics

*Cannabis wedding favors photograph beautifully and create real legal risk when hosts improvise sales, underage guests, or public consumption.*

Bay Area weddings already negotiate alcohol liability, venue rules, and uncle behavior. Add cannabis favors—mini pre-rolls, custom gummies, monogrammed tins—and hosts inherit another compliance checklist. California law can allow adult-to-adult gifts within limits; it does not turn a reception into a pop-up dispensary.

First principles: only adults 21 and older may receive cannabis. If your guest list includes teens, cousins in college dorms under 21, or vendors’ underage staff, a blanket favor bag is a problem. ID checks at a favor table feel weird; skipping them is worse. Many couples choose non-cannabis favors for that reason alone, or restrict cannabis gifts to a private after-party with a known adult list.

Second: gifts must be without compensation. Folding cannabis into ticketed ticket prices, mandatory “favor fees,” or cash bars that quietly include joints reintroduces sale dynamics. If a venue or planner proposes a packaged cannabis add-on, ask who holds the retailer license and how transfers occur. Unlicensed distribution dressed as romance is still unlicensed distribution.

Third: venue and hotel rules dominate. Many sites ban cannabis smoking regardless of state adult-use law. Outdoor garden ceremonies near downtown plazas do not become legal consumption zones after vows. Consume only where the property allows, privately, and never on public sidewalks between hotel and dinner. Smoke odor can violate contracts and trigger fees.

Fourth: dosage and labeling. Custom edibles without clear milligram labels are how guests overshoot and blame the couple. Prefer commercially labeled products from licensed retailers, sealed and given as true gifts in amounts that respect possession norms. Provide non-infused alternatives. Announce, somehow gracefully, that favors contain cannabis—do not let grandparents discover a gummy by taste.

Fifth: driving and overnight logistics. Impaired driving after a reception is a host’s nightmare whether the intoxicant is wine or weed. Encourage lodging, shuttles, and sober captains. Do not send guests onto freeways with a favor tin open in the cupholder.

Host liability is fact-specific; this article is not legal advice. Couples with elaborate cannabis plans should talk to a California attorney and to venue counsel early. The affordable alternative is simpler: a small, lawful gift for select adults 21+, or a group trip to a registered retailer before the wedding weekend where each adult buys their own products under ID rules.

San Jose celebrations can absolutely be cannabis-inclusive without becoming careless. Keep favors modest, age-gated, free, labeled, venue-compliant, and private. Your photos will still look stylish—and your Monday will be less likely to include angry emails from a venue manager or a guest who drove home impaired.

Local shoppers can keep this guidance beside the other essentials of adult-use life in San Jose: verify sellers on SJPD’s registered cannabis businesses list, keep receipts, store products away from children and pets, and consume only on private property where household rules allow. Those habits will not settle every debate, but they keep ordinary weekends inside the lawful, lower-drama lane this market was built to support.
